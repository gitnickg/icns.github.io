Services

Engagements are sized to your team. Most start with one area and grow from there.

  • Security architecture and reviews

    Bring security in early. I review new systems, cloud projects and major changes, assess the threats and risks, and give your team clear recommendations they can act on.

  • Identity and access

    Single sign‑on, multi-factor authentication and customer login done properly, including moves to identity providers such as Okta and standards like SAML, OAuth and OpenID Connect.

  • Security engineering and automation

    Hands-on help putting protections in place: vulnerability scanning, endpoint protection, central logging and monitoring, hardened servers, and automation that takes repetitive security work off your plate.

  • Incident response

    Someone to call when something goes wrong. I help you contain the problem, work out what happened and close the gap so it does not happen again.

  • Fintech and payments

    Answer the security reviews that banks, card networks and partners send you, check the vendors you rely on, and publish a security page that shows customers how you protect them. Infrastructure support for Bitcoin and digital asset operations too.

  • Security awareness

    Practical training and phishing simulations that help your staff spot and report suspicious messages, without making security feel like a chore.

How an engagement runs

No long contracts. Each step ends with something you can use.

  1. Free consultation

    A short call about what you run, what worries you and what you need to show customers and partners.

  2. Scope and quote

    A written scope with a fixed price, so you know exactly what is covered before work starts.

  3. Assessment

    Testing or review against the agreed scope, with urgent issues flagged to you as soon as they are found.

  4. Report and retest

    A plain-language report with prioritized fixes, a walkthrough call, and a retest once changes are in.
