Services

Engagements are sized to your team. Most start with one area and grow from there.

  • Security reviews

    A practical look at your systems, cloud setup or a new project before it launches. You get the issues that matter ranked by risk, plus help making sense of scanner output and penetration test results.

  • Security engineering and automation

    Hands-on help putting protections in place: automating repetitive security work, central logging and monitoring, endpoint protection rollouts, hardened server builds and single sign‑on.

  • Incident response

    Someone to call when something goes wrong. I help you contain the problem, work out what happened and close the gap so it does not happen again.

  • Fintech and payments

    Get ready for the security reviews banks, card networks and partners will send you, with clear answers and a public security page that builds trust. Infrastructure support for Bitcoin and digital asset operations too.

How an engagement runs

No long contracts. Each step ends with something you can use.

  1. Free consultation

    A short call about what you run, what worries you and what you need to show customers and partners.

  2. Scope and quote

    A written scope with a fixed price, so you know exactly what is covered before work starts.

  3. Assessment

    Testing or review against the agreed scope, with urgent issues flagged to you as soon as they are found.

  4. Report and retest

    A plain-language report with prioritized fixes, a walkthrough call, and a retest once changes are in.
