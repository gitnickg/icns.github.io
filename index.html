Services

Engagements are sized to your team. Most start with one area and grow from there.

  • Security testing

    Your web applications and internet-facing systems, tested the way an attacker would approach them. You get findings ranked by real-world risk, with steps to reproduce and fix each one.

  • Governance, risk and compliance

    Policies that match how your team actually works, a risk register you will keep using, and help preparing for frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 and PCI DSS.

  • Fintech and Bitcoin security

    Reviews for teams that handle payments or digital assets: key management, custody practices, wallet operations and the controls around them.

How an engagement runs

No long contracts. Each step ends with something you can use.

  1. Free consultation

    A short call about what you run, what worries you and what you need to prove to customers or auditors.

  2. Scope and quote

    A written scope with a fixed price, so you know exactly what is covered before work starts.

  3. Assessment

    Testing or review against the agreed scope, with urgent issues flagged to you as soon as they are found.

  4. Report and retest

    A plain-language report with prioritized fixes, a walkthrough call, and a retest once changes are in.
